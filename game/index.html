MISSION BRIEFING

You are the lone operator inserted into a hostile research facility. Fight through the complex, eliminate all hostiles, activate the master terminal in the north chamber, then reach the extraction pad in the yard and hold it until pickup.

Watch for supply drops: health, armor and ammo caches glow inside the facility. Enemy classes differ — rushers close fast, marksmen keep their distance, heavies soak damage. Hostiles take cover when hurt. So should you.

TEAM CLASH is a 5v5 round brawl: you and four AI operators against five hostiles. No respawns mid-round — wipe their team or fall trying. If the 100-second clock runs out the defenders take the round. First team to 6 rounds wins the match.
